LEGISLATIVE COUNSEL'S DIGESTSR 138, as introduced, Valladares. Digest KeyBill TextWHEREAS, California should prioritize policies that support safe communities, affordable housing, accessible high-quality health care, quality education, economic opportunity, individual liberty, and a government that is accountable to the people; andWHEREAS, Rising housing costs, inflation, and concerns about crime and public disorder have placed significant burdens on working families, seniors, small businesses, and communities throughout California; andWHEREAS, Public policy should promote economic growth, fiscal responsibility, effective public institutions, and the protection of individual liberty while ensuring taxpayer resources are used efficiently; andWHEREAS, Throughout the 20th and 21st centuries, governments organized around Marxist-Leninist and other socialist ideologies have, in numerous instances, evolved into authoritarian regimes characterized by the concentration of political and economic power, suppression of civil liberties, and widespread human rights abuses; andWHEREAS, Historians estimate that tens of millions of people perished under communist regimes through famine, political repression, forced labor, and state-sponsored violence in countries that include the former Soviet Union, the People’s Republic of China under Mao Zedong, Cambodia under the Khmer Rouge, North Korea, Cuba, and, more recently, Venezuela; andWHEREAS, These historical experiences demonstrate the dangers of concentrating excessive economic and political power in government at the expense of individual liberty, private enterprise, constitutional governance, and democratic accountability; andWHEREAS, America’s constitutional system is founded upon the principles of individual liberty, private property, free enterprise, limited government, and the rule of law, principles championed by the nation’s founders and embodied in the United States Constitution; andWHEREAS, The Democratic Socialists of America (DSA) is advancing policy positions advocating for the expansion of governmental control over the economy, including the seizure of private property, the governmental takeover of certain economic sectors, and unsustainable levels of public spending—positions inconsistent with the principles of limited government, free enterprise, and individual property rights. DSA advocates for the abolition of police and prisons, policies that threaten public safety and undermine law and order in our communities; andWHEREAS, The Senate finds that many of these proposals would risk increasing the tax burden on working families and employers, discourage private investment, reduce economic competitiveness, and make it more difficult to address California’s affordability crisis; andWHEREAS, The Senate believes affordability is best achieved by expanding the supply of housing, streamlining permitting, reducing unnecessary regulatory barriers, encouraging job creation, promoting responsible fiscal management, and supporting policies that enable Californians to keep more of their earnings; andWHEREAS, The Senate further believes that public safety is a core responsibility of government and that effective law enforcement, accountability throughout the criminal justice system, support for victims of crime, and evidence-based crime prevention strategies are essential to safe and thriving communities; now, therefore, be itResolved, by the Senate of the State of California, That it expresses its disagreement with the policy platform advanced by the Democratic Socialists of America, concluding that its approach would not effectively address California’s affordability challenges and would undermine economic growth, fiscal sustainability, and public safety, as well as limit innovation and individual liberty; and be it furtherResolved, That the Senate reaffirms its commitment to policies that achieve all of the following:Increase housing affordability by expanding the supply of homes, streamlining permitting, and reducing unnecessary bureaucratic obstacles;Reduce the cost of living through economic growth, responsible fiscal management, regulatory reform, and policies that allow families and small businesses to prosper;Promote safe communities by supporting effective law enforcement, accountability within the criminal justice system, victim services, and evidence-based crime prevention strategies while respecting constitutional rights;Foster an environment in which businesses can invest, innovate, create jobs, and contribute to long-term economic prosperity;Protect constitutional liberties, private property rights, and free enterprise as foundational principles of American self-government; andEncourage practical, results-oriented public policy that improves the daily lives of Californians through measurable outcomes rather than ideological approaches; and be it furtherResolved, that the Secretary of the Senate transmit copies of this resolution to the author for appropriate distribution.
