LEGISLATIVE COUNSEL'S DIGESTSR 134, as introduced, Wahab. Digest KeyBill TextWHEREAS, August 19 is recognized by Afghans throughout California and around the world as Afghan Independence Day; andWHEREAS, In 1919, following the Third Anglo-Afghan War and the Treaty of Rawalpindi, Afghanistan secured control over its foreign affairs from Great Britain, affirming its status as a free and independent nation; andWHEREAS, Afghanistan’s struggle for independence followed decades of resistance to British intervention, including three Anglo-Afghan wars, and during the First Anglo-Afghan War, British imperial forces suffered one of their worst military disasters of the 19th century, while the Third Anglo-Afghan War marked a defining moment in Afghanistan’s pursuit of full independence; andWHEREAS, Afghan Independence Day honors the Afghan people’s enduring commitment to sovereignty, self-determination, and the preservation of their national identity; andWHEREAS, Ahmad Shah Durrani, widely recognized as the founder of modern Afghanistan, established the Durrani Empire in 1747 and helped unite the region’s diverse peoples under an independent Afghan state; andWHEREAS, Situated at the crossroads of Central Asia, South Asia, and the Middle East, Afghanistan has for thousands of years served as a vital center of civilization, commerce, and cultural exchange along the historic Silk Road, connecting people, ideas, languages, and traditions across continents; andWHEREAS, Afghanistan is one of the world’s oldest continuously inhabited regions and is home to the ancient civilizations of Bactria and Gandhara, whose contributions to philosophy, art, architecture, trade, and scholarship helped shape the development of Asia and the broader world; andWHEREAS, Afghanistan’s rich intellectual and spiritual heritage is associated with some of history’s most influential thinkers, poets, and traditions, including Jalal al-Din Muhammad Rumi, whose poetry continues to inspire millions through its timeless messages of love, compassion, and humanity, and Zarathustra, also known as Zoroaster, whose teachings profoundly influenced religious and ethical thought across civilizations; andWHEREAS, Afghanistan’s cultural legacy includes internationally recognized treasures such as the Bamiyan Valley, ancient cities, archaeological sites, and centuries of artistic and literary achievement that continue to inspire people around the world; andWHEREAS, Throughout history, the Afghan people have demonstrated remarkable resilience, preserving their languages, customs, and cultural heritage through periods of both prosperity and hardship; andWHEREAS, The Senate recognizes the extraordinary courage, resilience, and perseverance of Afghan women and girls, who continue to advocate for education, equality, dignity, and fundamental human rights despite extraordinary oppression, and acknowledges that the promise of a truly free Afghanistan cannot be fully realized until its women and girls are free; andWHEREAS, Many Afghan Americans living in California arrived after fleeing war, persecution, and humanitarian crises, bringing with them an enduring commitment to freedom, democracy, education, entrepreneurship, public service, and community leadership; andWHEREAS, Afghan Independence Day provides an opportunity to celebrate Afghanistan’s rich history, recognize the resilience and achievements of the Afghan people, honor the contributions of Afghan Americans, and foster greater appreciation for the nation’s enduring cultural legacy; now, therefore, be itResolved by the Senate of the State of California, That the Senate recognizes August 19, 2026, as Afghan Independence Day and joins Afghan communities throughout California in celebrating their history, heritage, resilience, culture, and lasting contributions to the Golden State; and be it furtherResolved, That the Senate honors the enduring spirit of the Afghan people and recognizes that the ideals of independence, liberty, and self-determination are strengthened when every person, including women and girls, can live in freedom, dignity, and equality; and be it furtherResolved, That the Senate encourages all Californians to learn more about Afghanistan’s history, the significance of Afghan Independence Day, and the rich traditions, achievements, and contributions of Afghan Americans; and be it furtherResolved, That the Secretary of the Senate transmit copies of this resolution to the author for appropriate distribution.
