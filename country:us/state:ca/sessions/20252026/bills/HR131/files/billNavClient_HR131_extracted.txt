LEGISLATIVE COUNSEL'S DIGESTHR 131, as introduced, Muratsuchi. Digest KeyBill TextWHEREAS, Cancer remains one of the leading causes of death among people of all ages, and early education and awareness are critical tools in the fight against this disease; andWHEREAS, Young people, including students, benefit immensely from increased awareness about cancer risk factors, prevention strategies, early detection methods, and available resources for support and treatment; andWHEREAS, Education can empower students to make healthier lifestyle choices, such as avoiding tobacco, practicing sun safety, eating a balanced diet, and being physically active, that may significantly reduce their risk of developing cancer; andWHEREAS, Many forms of cancer, when detected early, have higher rates of successful treatment and survival, highlighting the importance of informing students about early warning signs and the value of routine medical care; andWHEREAS, Student Cancer Education, Detection, and Prevention Week will serve as an opportunity for schools, colleges, community organizations, health professionals, and families to come together to promote cancer education, encourage preventative behaviors, and support those affected by the disease; andWHEREAS, Engaging students in age-appropriate discussions about cancer not only fosters awareness but also helps reduce stigma and build compassion, resilience, and community action; andWHEREAS, It is in the interest of the State of California to support the health and well-being of its youth by fostering a culture of proactive health education; now, therefore, be itResolved by the Assembly of the State of California, That the Assembly proclaims October 11, 2026, through October 17, 2026, inclusive, as Student Cancer Education, Detection, and Prevention Week; and be it furtherResolved, That the Legislature encourages middle schools, high schools, colleges, public health agencies, pediatricians, and community health providers to promote comprehensive educational campaigns on topics related to cancer prevention and detection, including risk factors, warning signs, evidence-based prevention strategies, and the importance of timely screenings and diagnostic evaluations; and be it furtherResolved, That the Chief Clerk of the Assembly transmit copies of this resolution to the author for appropriate distribution.
