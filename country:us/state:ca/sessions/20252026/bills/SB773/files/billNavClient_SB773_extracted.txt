LEGISLATIVE COUNSEL'S DIGESTSB 773, as introduced, Ashby. Board of Registered Nursing: advisory committees.Existing law, the Nursing Practice Act, establishes the Board of Registered Nursing to license and regulate the practice of nursing. Existing law authorizes the board, with the permission of the Director of the Department of Consumer Affairs, to form advisory committees to advise the board on the implementation of the act.This bill would remove the requirement that the board receive permission from the director to form those advisory committees.Digest Key Vote: MAJORITY Appropriation: NO Fiscal Committee: YES Local Program: NO  Bill TextThe people of the State of California do enact as follows:SECTION 1. Section 2710.5 of the Business and Professions Code is amended to read:2710.5. The board, with permission of the Director of the Department of Consumer Affairs, board may form advisory committees to advise the board on the implementation of this chapter. Members of such advisory committees shall be entitled to a per diem and expenses as provided in Section 103.
